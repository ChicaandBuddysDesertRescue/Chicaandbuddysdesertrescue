♡ Who We Are

Chica & Buddy's Desert Rescue is a small, heart-led Arizona rescue. We keep dogs and cats out of overcrowded kennels and unsafe situations by placing them directly into loving foster homes and carefully screened adoptive homes.

Our Mission

To honor Chica and Buddy by saying "yes" to the lives most at risk: medical cases, abandoned pets, seniors, and those left behind — and to give them safety and love.

Shelter pulls
Medical & special needs
Owner support & rehoming
Spay/neuter advocacy

What Makes Us Different

100% foster-based. Transparent. Committed for life with a no-judgment return promise. We focus on the animals that are running out of time.

Home-based evaluations
Behavior & decompression support
Lifetime safety net

🏡 Foster

Fostering opens a kennel door for the next dog or cat in line. We’ve got you; you’ve got them.

What We Provide

  • Approved vetting & medical
  • Supplies when available
  • Support every step of the way

What You Provide

  • Safe indoor space
  • Love, patience, consistency
  • Photos & updates so we can network them

High-Need Fosters

  • Large & bully breeds
  • Moms & babies / bottle kittens
  • Shy or shutdown dogs who need time
