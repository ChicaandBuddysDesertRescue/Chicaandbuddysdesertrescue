♡ Who We Are

Chica & Buddy's Desert Rescue is a small, heart-led Arizona rescue. We work with shelters, outreach teams, and families in crisis to keep dogs and cats out of overcrowded kennels and off the streets. Every animal in our care is treated like our own: fully vetted, loved, and carefully matched so they never end up homeless again.

Our Mission

To honor Chica and Buddy by saying "yes" to the lives most at risk: medical cases, abandoned pets, seniors, and those left behind — and to place them into safe, committed, loving homes.

Shelter pulls
Medical & special needs
Owner support & rehoming
Spay/neuter advocacy

What Makes Us Different

100% foster-based, no cold kennels. We stay transparent, support adopters and fosters, and stand by our animals for life with a no-questions-asked return promise.

🏡 Foster

Fosters are the shield between an animal and the shelter door. Even a short-term foster can literally save a life.

What We Provide

  • All approved vetting & medical
  • Food & supplies when available
  • Ongoing support for behavior & decompression

What You Provide

  • Safe indoor space in Arizona
  • Love, structure, and patience
  • Photos/updates so we can find their family

High-Need Fosters

  • Large & bully breeds
  • Underage kittens & moms with litters
  • Scared/neglected dogs needing time
